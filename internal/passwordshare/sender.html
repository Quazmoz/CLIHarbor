CLIHarbor · One-time password transfer

Send a password

Someone using CLIHarbor asked you for one password. It travels directly to their computer over your local network and can be sent only once.

Receiving computer:

  1. Ask the recipient to read you their receiving address by phone, in person or another trusted channel. It must match the address above.
  2. Check that your browser shows a secure connection. If it shows a certificate warning, stop and tell the recipient.
  3. Enter the password and send it.

Single line, up to 4,096 bytes. Nothing is saved on this page or in CLIHarbor history.

This link accepts one password and expires five minutes after the recipient created it. Anyone holding the link could send a password, so the recipient should confirm with you that it came from you.
